2. In short

The app works without an account or sign-up. Your progress is stored only on your device. The app does not send any personal data to us.

3. Your progress

Your progress (game state, settings, the advantages you bought and the identifiers of completed purchases) is stored inside the app on your device. It is included in your device or iCloud backup, which Apple manages according to your settings. We have no access to it. To delete it: Settings › “Delete progress”, or delete the app.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive payment or account details. The app only receives Apple's confirmation of a purchase (the product and an identifier of the transaction) and keeps the identifier on your device so that every purchase is credited exactly once. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. Ratings

The app may show Apple's rating dialog. You rate directly with Apple; we only see the ratings that are published in the App Store.

6. No ads, no analytics, no tracking

The app currently contains no advertising, no analytics and no crash reporting by third parties, and it does not track you across apps or websites of other companies.

7. E-mail to us

If you write to us, we use your e-mail address and your message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We delete them once your request is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them.

8. This website

These pages are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company). When you open them, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver and protect the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where data is transferred to the USA, GitHub states that it relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from third parties (the font is part of the site). GitHub's privacy statement: docs.github.com.

9. Children

The game is suitable for all ages and collects no personal data.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As the app sends us no data about you, this mainly concerns e-mails you send us.

11. Changes

When new features arrive (for example rewarded videos or crash reports), we update this policy before they go live.
