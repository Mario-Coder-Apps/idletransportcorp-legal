2. In short

The app works without an account or sign-up. Your progress is stored only on your device. We do not receive any personal data ourselves. Partners deliver the optional videos and receive crash reports and – in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland only with your consent – game statistics; Google and GameAnalytics use identifiers of your device for this (section 6).

3. Your progress

Your progress (game state, settings, the advantages you bought and the identifiers of completed purchases) is stored inside the app on your device. It is included in your device or iCloud backup, which Apple manages according to your settings. We have no access to it. To delete it: Settings › “Delete progress”, or delete the app.

4. In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive payment or account details. The app only receives Apple's confirmation of a purchase (the product and an identifier of the transaction) and keeps the identifier on your device so that every purchase is credited exactly once. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

5. Ratings

The app may show Apple's rating dialog. You rate directly with Apple; we only see the ratings that are published in the App Store.

6. Services of partners

The app uses three services of partners, described below: rewarded videos (Google AdMob), crash reports (Sentry) and game statistics (GameAnalytics). Google and GameAnalytics work with identifiers of your device, as described below; we do not combine them with other data about you.

Rewarded videos (Google AdMob)

The app starts Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland) when it opens and loads the rewarded videos in advance, so that a video is ready when you want one. In the EEA and the UK it asks for your consent first (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) and starts AdMob only after your choice; you can change it in the settings. Google then processes device information, your IP address and an approximate location derived from it – even if you never watch a video – and your interactions with the ads. Google uses the advertising identifier of your device (IDFA) only if you allow it in Apple's tracking prompt. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Crash reports (Sentry)

If the app crashes or runs into an error, it sends a report to Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.; data stored in the EU): device model, iOS and app version, the error and the last steps in the game. The report contains no name, contact or advertising identifier. Legal basis: our legitimate interest in a working game (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).

Game statistics (GameAnalytics)

The app sends game events (for example tutorial steps, sites opened, purchases by product and price, videos watched) with device information (model, iOS version, language) to GameAnalytics ApS (Denmark), so that we can see where the game is too hard or too easy. GameAnalytics links these events to identifiers of your device: a user ID it creates, the identifier for vendors (IDFV) and – only if you allow it in Apple's tracking prompt – the advertising identifier (IDFA). We use the statistics only to improve the game, not for advertising. In the EEA, the UK and Switzerland the app sends them only with your consent – the game asks once (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR); elsewhere they are on. Either way you can switch them off at any time: Settings › Play statistics. GameAnalytics' privacy policy: gameanalytics.com/privacy.

7. E-mail to us

If you write to us, we use your e-mail address and your message to answer you (Art. 6(1)(b) and (f) GDPR). We delete them once your request is settled, unless the law requires us to keep them.

8. This website

These pages are served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc., a Microsoft company). When you open them, GitHub processes technically necessary data such as your IP address in server logs to deliver and protect the site (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). Where data is transferred to the USA, GitHub states that it relies on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework and standard contractual clauses. This website sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads nothing from third parties (the font is part of the site). GitHub's privacy statement: docs.github.com.

9. Children

The game is suitable for all ages. It never asks for a name, contact details or an account; the partner services above work without them.

10. Your rights

You have the right to access, rectification, erasure, restriction of processing, data portability and to object (Art. 15–21 GDPR), and to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority. As the app sends us no data about you, this mainly concerns e-mails you send us.

11. Changes

When new features arrive (for example rewarded videos or crash reports), we update this policy before they go live.

6. Dienste von Partnern

Die App nutzt drei Dienste von Partnern, die unten beschrieben sind: Belohnungsvideos (Google AdMob), Absturzberichte (Sentry) und eine Spielstatistik (GameAnalytics). Google und GameAnalytics arbeiten mit Kennungen deines Geräts, wie unten beschrieben; wir verknüpfen sie nicht mit anderen Daten über dich.

Belohnungsvideos (Google AdMob)

Die App startet Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Irland) beim Öffnen und lädt die Belohnungsvideos vorab, damit ein Video bereitsteht, wenn du eines möchtest. Im EWR und im Vereinigten Königreich fragt sie vorher deine Einwilligung ab (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO) und startet AdMob erst nach deiner Wahl; du kannst sie in den Einstellungen ändern. Google verarbeitet dann Geräteinformationen, deine IP-Adresse und einen daraus abgeleiteten ungefähren Standort – auch wenn du nie ein Video ansiehst – sowie deine Interaktionen mit der Werbung. Die Werbe-ID deines Geräts (IDFA) nutzt Google nur, wenn du es in der Tracking-Abfrage von Apple erlaubst. Datenschutzerklärung von Google: policies.google.com/privacy.

Absturzberichte (Sentry)

Stürzt die App ab oder tritt ein Fehler auf, sendet sie einen Bericht an Sentry (Functional Software, Inc.; Speicherung in der EU): Gerätemodell, iOS- und App-Version, den Fehler und die letzten Schritte im Spiel. Der Bericht enthält keinen Namen, keine Kontaktdaten und keine Werbe-ID. Rechtsgrundlage: unser berechtigtes Interesse an einem funktionierenden Spiel (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. f DSGVO).

Spielstatistik (GameAnalytics)

Die App sendet Spielereignisse (z. B. Tutorial-Schritte, eröffnete Standorte, Käufe nach Produkt und Preis, angesehene Videos) mit Geräteinformationen (Modell, iOS-Version, Sprache) an GameAnalytics ApS (Dänemark), damit wir sehen, wo das Spiel zu schwer oder zu leicht ist. GameAnalytics verknüpft diese Ereignisse mit Kennungen deines Geräts: einer selbst erzeugten Nutzer-ID, der Kennung für Anbieter (IDFV) und – nur wenn du es in der Tracking-Abfrage von Apple erlaubst – der Werbe-ID (IDFA). Wir nutzen die Statistik nur, um das Spiel zu verbessern, nicht für Werbung. Im EWR, im Vereinigten Königreich und in der Schweiz sendet die App sie nur mit deiner Einwilligung – das Spiel fragt einmal (Art. 6 Abs. 1 lit. a DSGVO); anderswo ist sie an. In jedem Fall kannst du sie jederzeit abschalten: Einstellungen › Spielstatistik. Datenschutzerklärung von GameAnalytics: gameanalytics.com/privacy.
